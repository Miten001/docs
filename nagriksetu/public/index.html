Report civic problems. Track their progress.

NagrikSetu is a civic engagement platform that empowers the citizens of Nagpur to report local civic problems — from potholes and garbage to water and traffic issues — and to follow their complaints from submission through to resolution.

Submit a complaint in minutes and receive a unique Complaint ID. Use that ID at any time to check the current status and details of your report.

Report a Problem Track a Complaint

Report Problems

Tell us about civic issues in your area — potholes, garbage, water, traffic, and more — with a description, location, and an optional photo.

Track Your Complaint

Every submission gets a unique Complaint ID. Enter it on the Track page to see the latest status and details of your report.

Civic Dashboard

View an at-a-glance overview of reported problems grouped by category and by their current resolution status.

Transparent & Shared

Complaints are stored centrally so the same, consistent information is available to every citizen across all devices.
