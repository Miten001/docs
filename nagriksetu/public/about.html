About NagrikSetu

Connecting the citizens of Nagpur with the civic services they rely on.

Our Mission

NagrikSetu exists to make civic problem-solving simple, transparent, and accountable. Our mission is to give every citizen of Nagpur a straightforward way to report the everyday problems that affect their neighbourhoods — and to follow those reports until they are resolved.

By bringing complaints together in one shared, consistent place, we help citizens and civic services see the same information and work together toward a cleaner, safer, better-functioning city.

How NagrikSetu Helps Citizens

A Simple Way to Report

Report potholes, garbage, water, traffic, and other civic problems in minutes, with a description, a location, and an optional photo.

Transparency Through Tracking

Every complaint receives a unique Complaint ID, so you can check its status and details whenever you like.

A Shared Record

Complaints are stored centrally, so the same information is available to every citizen across all devices.

Insight at a Glance

The civic dashboard summarises reported problems by category and status, giving the community a clear overview.
